# Self-Study Tracker - Written by me, not AI


My personal version of this [template](https://github.com/JStadl/template-self-study-tracker). It contains the data that is then used to show a webpage of my progress with online courses I have pursued for my leisure. You can see the page [here](https://jstadl.github.io/self-study-tracker/). 

## Why
There are some absolutely amazing courses available for free online. I find great enjoyment in taking these, when I have the time. However, I find there are some niggles:

1. You don't have as much encouragement as you would taking them in person, or for money, as you don't have a physical person to encourage you/force you to do the work, and you don't get a prize (certificate) at the end.
2. The admin work involved (scheduling, bookkeeping, timeline planning) is not done for you, and is boring.
3. It is quite tempting to look at solutions prematurely, stunting your learning. Even worse if you want your work marked for you.

This tool is my starting attempt at alleviating these issues:

1. By making it public, there is some pressure, and the ticking things off nature is satisfying enough to also play a role in encouragement.
2. This tool, or rather the AI you would prompt to create the config for a course you'd like to start in addition to this tool, does this boring stuff for you for little effort.
3. This adds another barrier to cheating, which helps discourage somewhat, and I hope to later add further AI marking integration, or similar.
